Google Places — what it costs us

A planning reference for how much a trip's place data costs, what we pull today vs. everything Google offers, when we get re-charged, and how our caching keeps revisits near $0. Numbers use our code's cost model (google.ts) and Google's published SKU tiers.

Status: we've spent $0 so far. No place has been crawled yet — the sample trips were seeded directly, so the crawl never fired. Google is configured, not used. Turning it on is a deliberate switch.
The 3 cost buckets

Where the money goes (and doesn't)

① Facts

~$0.020

per place, ONCE.

Name, rating, hours, phone, website, photo refs. Stored in our places cache (ID forever, facts ~30 days).

Revisit → $0. Read from our DB, no Google call.

② Photos

~$0.007

per photo, when first shown.

Refs stored free; the resolved image URL is temporary. We reuse it for ~1h (our cache) + resolve lazily (hero only).

Revisit → $0 within the window; a stray re-resolve is a half-cent.

③ Reviews

~$0.025

per open, each fresh view.

ToS forbids storing review text, so it can't be cached. Fetched only when you open the reviews section.

Recurring — but only when someone actually opens reviews, then session-cached.

A Place Details call is billed at the highest tier of any field in it. Our crawl mask tops out at Enterprise (rating/hours) and deliberately excludes reviews to stay off the Atmosphere tier. Photos and reviews are separate SKUs, fetched on their own.

The rules

When do we (re)crawl?

  • Crawl once per place, keyed by Place ID, and shared across every trip that references it — the 2nd trip to use a place pays $0.
  • Facts live in our places collection: Place ID cached forever, other facts on a ~30-day TTL. A revisit reads our DB — no Google call. We only re-crawl facts if the 30-day TTL has lapsed and the place is viewed again.
  • Photos: the reference is stored forever; the resolved URL is reused for ~1h (our cache), resolved lazily and hero-only. No per-render charge.
  • Reviews: never stored (ToS). Fetched only when the reviews section is opened, then session-cached. This is the one bucket that re-charges on a genuinely new view.
  • The switch: a Places on/off toggle — off means bundled stock and $0; on means real photos + info. You decide when to spend.
Estimates are planning figures from our cost model + Google's published SKU structure; Google's pricing and free-tier terms change — verify at mapsplatform.google.com/pricing. Google also grants a monthly free allotment per SKU; small trips may land within it. No review text or photo bytes are ever stored — only IDs, facts (TTL) and photo references.
