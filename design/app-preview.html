What’s fixed here

Approach 02 · SHIPPED

Map-first

The cinematic night map is the whole app. Every screen is a rich glass sheet sliding over the live map — you always see where you are and where you’re going. Dense, spatial, information-forward. Same palette, type, companion and circular dock.

Live build (2026-08-30): the map is now a real 3D vector basemap (OpenFreeMap dark, keyless) with Google-style grape building extrusions + tilt, and is fully interactive — pan, pinch/zoom, +/− controls, and tappable pins that open an info popup. Sheets are night glass and collapse via the drag handle. The 3D walking mannequin lives only on the Generating screen (below), not on the map. These static frames illustrate layout & content; the shipped map renders live 3D.
