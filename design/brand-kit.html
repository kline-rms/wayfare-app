01 · Our story

Planning a trip shouldn't feel like work.

Wayfare started with a very specific frustration: a family in BGC trying to plan a few days across Old Manila — juggling spreadsheets, group chats, twelve browser tabs, and a map app that never knew where the day actually started.

Every planner assumed you already knew the stops. None of them answered the real question: from my door, in this city, with these people and this budget — what does a good day actually look like, in order?

So we built the opposite. You tell Wayfare where you start and who you're travelling with. It proposes real routes, honest peso ranges, and a reason behind every pick — then draws the whole day on one living map and nudges you from stop to stop. Born in Manila, built for anywhere.

02 · What drives us
Mission

Turn any trip into a day you can just walk.

We take the planning weight off travellers — routing, timing, costing, and sequencing every day so people spend their attention on the trip, not the logistics.

Vision

One map for every journey on earth.

A world where opening Wayfare means your whole trip is already spatial, live and door-to-door — as natural as glancing out a window to see where you're going.

Promise

Confident, never bossy.

Every plan is editable, every recommendation is explained, and nothing happens you didn't choose. Wayfare guides — you decide.

03 · The mark

The route-W

The mark is a W drawn as a dashed walking route — the same amber footpath that threads every Wayfare map — ending in a coral destination pin. It says the whole brand in one glyph: a journey, on foot, arriving somewhere worth it.

✓ Keep the coral dot at the final peak
✓ Give it room — clearspace = the corner radius
✕ Don't recolor the route off amber
✕ Don't fill the dashes into a solid W

06 · Voice & tone

Talk like a friend who's been there.

✕ Not
"Itinerary generation complete. 3 proposals available."
✓ "Your trip, in order — pick a plan."
✕ Not
"Error: unable to route selected waypoints."
✓ "Couldn't route that one — want to swap the last stop?"
✕ Not
"Configure notification preferences."
✓ "Nudge me before each stop."
✕ Not
"Optimal departure: 14:00."
✓ "Leave in 1 hour · 6 min walk · ☀ 31°."

Warm

Second person, active voice, everyday words. "You", not "the user."

Specific

Real numbers, real places. Distance, time, and ₱ over vague promises.

Confident

Recommend clearly, explain briefly, and always leave the choice with the traveller.
